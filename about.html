Mission Statement

"To support and encourage honest and scholarly historical research on the Seventh-day Adventist movement from its beginning to modern time."

After gathering informally for several years at the American Historical Association (AHA) annual sessions, history teachers in Adventist higher education formally organized into the Association of Seventh-day Adventist Historians (ASDAH) in 1973. ASDAH has evolved into an open community of scholars and laypersons who share the common interest of fostering research not only on Seventh-day Adventist history but all aspects of history.
